# Your first hour

Written for someone who has never done this before. Nothing here needs code.

---

## What you are actually building

A folder on your computer full of plain text files, and an AI that can read and
write them.

That is genuinely it. No website, no database, no subscription beyond the one you
already bought. The folder works with the wifi off. If you stopped using Claude
tomorrow, every note would still open in any text editor on earth.

**The reason this matters:** an AI with no memory is a very smart stranger. Every
conversation starts over. You re-explain your market, your buy box, your partners,
what happened on that deal last spring. The vault is the memory. Once it exists,
you stop repeating yourself and the answers get sharper because they are about
your actual business.

---

## Step 1: make the folder

Unzip `second-brain-starter.zip` into your home folder. You get a folder called:

```
second-brain
```

Lowercase, with the hyphen. It is already named correctly, so there is nothing to
rename. Everyone in class uses the same name, which is why instructions will work on
your screen without translating.

---

## Step 2: open it in Obsidian

Obsidian → **Open folder as vault** → pick `second-brain`.

You will see the folders. Click into `daily/` and open the file there. That is what
a day looks like.

Obsidian is just a nice window onto the folder. The files are the real thing.

---

## Step 3: let Claude see the folder

In the Claude desktop app, give it access to your `second-brain` folder when it
asks. Say yes to reading your files. **That permission is the whole point.** In a
browser tab Claude can only talk. With folder access it can do the work.

Quick check that it worked. Ask:

> what's in my daily folder?

If it lists the file, you are connected. If it says it cannot reach the folder,
that is a permission, not a limit, and it is a two minute fix. Bring it to the
power hour.

---

## Step 4: the interview

Say:

> let's set up my second brain

It will ask you about yourself and your business, one question at a time, for
about fifteen minutes. Then it writes what it learned into your vault.

**Answer honestly and specifically.** "I do wholesale" is a weak answer. "I do
wholesale in three counties around Tampa, mostly from cold calls, and I close two
to four a month" is one it can actually use.

The most important question it asks is the thing you would pay to never do again.
Take your time on that one.

---

## A habit worth starting now

Begin every session with:

> read CLAUDE.md in my vault and follow it

Two seconds, and it guarantees your standing instructions are actually in play rather
than sitting in a file nobody opened.

## Step 5: put something real in

Open today's file in `daily/` and paste in something from your actual week. A
seller conversation, a list you have not worked, a deal that is stuck.

Messy is fine. Half sentences are fine. Do not clean it up.

Then ask:

> what's in my daily note, and what should I do about it?

That is the loop. Capture, ask, act. Everything else in the class is a variation
on it.

---

## What to expect

**It will get things wrong.** It will confidently state something that is not
true, especially about things that were decided on a phone call and never written
down. That is not a malfunction, it is the nature of the thing. Your job is to
know which questions it had no way to answer.

**It gets better as the vault fills.** Week one it is a smart assistant. Month
three it is something else, because by then it knows things nobody else wrote down.

**You cannot break it.** Every file is text. Nothing here can delete your CRM, spend
your money, or email a client. The worst case is a messy folder.

---

## Words you will hear today

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Vault** | The folder. That is all. |
| **Skill** | A note that tells Claude how to do one job. Plain text. You can write one. |
| **CLAUDE.md** | The file it reads first every session. Your standing instructions. |
| **Hook** | Something that runs automatically, every time, no exceptions. |
| **Context** | Its working memory for this conversation. It is finite, which is why the vault matters. |
| **MCP** | A connector to an outside system, like your CRM. |

If you hear a word that is not on this list and it is not explained, stop and ask.
Somebody else in the room has the same question.
