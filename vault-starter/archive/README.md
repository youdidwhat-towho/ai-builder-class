# archive/

Passive memory. Everything in here is still yours and still searchable. It
just does not show up when you ask what you are working on.

## Active and passive

Your vault has two speeds.

**Active memory** is every other folder. When you ask "what am I working
on", "who should I call", "what's open", the answer comes from here.

**Passive memory** is this folder. When you ask "have I ever", "what did I
do with", "back when", or you name something by name, the answer can come
from here too.

Same vault, two retrieval modes. That is the whole idea. A vault with only
one mode gets worse every week it grows, because month six searches return
four things that were true in March and one that is true now.

## What lands here

Notes whose subject has concluded. A closed deal. A decision that was made
and acted on. A project that shipped. A buyer who moved away.

Nothing gets deleted and nothing arrives here by itself. Say `maintain`
every couple of weeks and you will get a list of candidates with a reason
next to each one. You pick.

## Shape

The folder it came from is preserved, so `deals/1247-oak-st.md` archives to
`archive/deals/1247-oak-st.md`. Each archived note carries one line at the
bottom saying when it moved and why.

## What never comes here

`daily/` is never pruned. A dated note is not stale when it stops being
touched, it is finished. Those notes are the record of what actually
happened, which is the one thing this system exists to keep.
