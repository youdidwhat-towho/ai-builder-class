# people/

One file per person you have a real working relationship with.

**The bar is relationship, not transaction.** Somebody you traded two emails
with does not need a file. Somebody whose way of operating you need in your
head before the next call does.

Write what actually matters about them: how they behave, what they care
about, what went sideways last time. Not their title. Their title is on
LinkedIn; how they negotiate is not.

Keep a dated log at the bottom. Append only.
